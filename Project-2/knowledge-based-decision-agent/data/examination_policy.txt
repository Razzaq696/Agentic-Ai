# University Examination and Assessment Policy

## Examination Hall Regulations
Final semester examinations are conducted during the designated exam period at the conclusion of each semester. All students must present a valid university-issued student identification card upon entering the examination room. Students arriving more than 15 minutes after the scheduled examination start time will not be admitted.

## Academic Integrity and Dishonesty
Possession of unauthorized electronic devices, textbooks, cheat sheets, or communication with peers during an examination constitutes academic misconduct. Any student caught cheating or plagiarizing will receive an immediate score of zero on the examination and will be referred to the Academic Disciplinary Board for potential semester suspension.

## Missed Examinations and Make-Up Procedures
Absence from a scheduled midterm or final examination results in a score of zero unless the absence is caused by a severe documented medical emergency or bereavement. Students seeking a make-up examination must file a formal petition supported by evidence to the Academic Dean within forty-eight (48) hours of the missed exam. Approved make-up exams must be completed within two weeks of the original exam date.
