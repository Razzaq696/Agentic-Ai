# University Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy

## Merit-Based Scholarship Eligibility
The University Academic Excellence Scholarship provides a 75% tuition waiver for undergraduate students enrolled full-time (minimum 15 credit hours per semester). To qualify, continuing students must achieve and maintain a cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 3.75 or higher and have no active disciplinary infractions.

## Need-Based Financial Grants
Need-based financial grants cover between 25% and 50% of tuition costs for students demonstrating verified financial hardship. Applicants must submit official household income tax returns and the Student Financial Assistance Form.

## Application Process and Annual Deadlines
Scholarship and financial aid applications open on August 1 and close promptly on October 15 for the fall semester, and open on December 1 closing on January 15 for the spring semester. Late submissions are not considered. Scholarships are renewed on an annual basis provided GPA and credit hour benchmarks are satisfied.
