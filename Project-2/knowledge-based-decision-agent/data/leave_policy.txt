# University Student Leave of Absence Policy

## Temporary Leave of Absence
Enrolled degree-seeking students who need to interrupt their studies for personal, medical, or professional reasons may apply for a formal Temporary Leave of Absence through the Office of Student Services.

## Leave Duration and Limitations
A standard leave of absence is granted for a duration of one (1) semester or up to a maximum of two (2) consecutive semesters. Students who do not return to active enrollment at the conclusion of the approved leave period will be formally withdrawn and must apply for academic readmission.

## Medical Leave Documentation and Re-entry
Requests for medical leave must include a signed recommendation from a licensed healthcare provider detailing the medical condition. Prior to resuming classes, students returning from medical leave must provide a healthcare provider clearance letter certifying their fitness to resume academic studies.
