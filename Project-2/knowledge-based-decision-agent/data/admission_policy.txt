# University Admission Policy

## General Admission Requirements
Prospective undergraduate students must submit an official high school transcript demonstrating a minimum cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale. 

## Standardized Testing and Documentation
Applicants are required to submit standardized test scores (SAT minimum score of 1150 or ACT composite score of 24). In addition, two academic letters of recommendation and a personal statement of purpose (500 to 750 words) must accompany the online application.

## Application Deadlines
- Fall Semester Early Action Deadline: November 15
- Fall Semester Regular Decision Deadline: February 1
- Spring Semester Application Deadline: October 1

Applications submitted after the regular deadline are reviewed on a rolling basis subject to available program capacity.
