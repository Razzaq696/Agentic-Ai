# University Tuition and Fee Payment Policy

## Payment Deadlines
All semester tuition, laboratory fees, activity fees, and health insurance charges must be settled in full by the end of the second week of each academic semester (the formal tuition payment deadline).

## Installment Payment Plans
Students experiencing temporary payment constraints may enroll in the University Deferred Installment Plan prior to the tuition deadline. The plan splits total semester fees into three equal installments due in Month 1, Month 2, and Month 3 of the semester, accompanied by a non-refundable $35 administrative setup fee.

## Late Fees and Registration Holds
A mandatory late payment penalty of $100 is assessed on any unpaid balance starting from the first business day following the payment deadline. Accounts remaining in default for more than thirty (30) calendar days will be placed on Financial Hold, preventing course registration, transcript issuance, and graduation clearance.
